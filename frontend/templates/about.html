About the project

The Smart Waste Management System helps people identify waste categories, dispose of waste correctly and arrange collection. A transfer-learning image classifier (MobileNetV2) predicts one of five categories: plastic, paper, metal, glass or organic. Every result is stored so users and administrators can follow trends over time.

Built with Flask, PostgreSQL, TensorFlow, OpenCV, Bootstrap and Chart.js, and packaged with Docker for easy deployment.
